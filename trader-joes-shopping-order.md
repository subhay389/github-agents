# Trader Joe's Shopping Aisle Order

Use this preferred Trader Joe's shopping order whenever organizing the user's grocery or shopping list. Keep items grouped by the store sections below and present them in this sequence. The order is based on the user's usual Trader Joe's layout; exact aisle placement can vary by location.

1. **Produce**
   - Fresh fruits and vegetables, herbs, lemons, garlic, etc.

2. **Meat**
   - Fresh meat, chicken, sausages, and other refrigerated raw proteins.

3. **Bread**
   - Bread, pita, tortillas, wraps, and bakery items.

4. **Milk & dairy**
   - Milk, eggs, yogurt, cream, and other general dairy/refrigerated staples.

5. **Pasta & pantry**
   - Pasta, noodles, sauces, oils, seasonings, spices, and shelf-stable cooking ingredients.

6. **Frozen foods**
   - Frozen meals, vegetables, fruit, and other frozen items.

7. **Cheese, pesto & salsa**
   - Keep cheese, pesto, salsa, dips, and closely related refrigerated specialty items together in this section.

8. **Juices**
   - Juices and other refrigerated beverages.

9. **Snacks**
   - Chips, crackers, nuts, sweets, and snack foods.

10. **Wine**
    - Wines and other alcoholic beverages.

## How to apply this note
- Reorganize every grocery list into the sections above, in exactly this order.
- Put each item in the best-fitting section based on its product type, not the order in which it was mentioned.
- Keep related ingredients together where practical, but do not move an item to a different section just to group a recipe's ingredients.
- If an item could fit more than one section, choose the section that best matches how Trader Joe's typically merchandises it.
- Do not invent extra items. If adding ingredients for a recipe, include them in the appropriate sections above.
- If a section has no items, omit it rather than showing an empty heading.
- Treat this as the user's preferred shopping route, while allowing for store-to-store layout differences.
